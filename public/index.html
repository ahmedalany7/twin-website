What is Twin?

Twin – Our Love World is a private app for exactly two people (a couple). There is no social network, no public profile and no sharing with strangers: what you add inside the space is visible to you and your partner only, and your space syncs between your two phones.

  • 📸Memories

    Photos and days you do not want to lose, arranged the way you lived them.

  • 💌Messages

    Words for today and for a day further away, delivered to your partner on time.

  • 🌸Our love world

    A small world that grows with every moment you add.

  • 🎮Games

    Light challenges and playful moments that bring you closer.

And more

  • 🧩Puzzle together

    A new photo every day, shown to you both at the same moment; whoever puts it together first wins.

  • 💭Thinking of you

    One tap that tells your partner they are on your mind.

  • ✉️Open When

    Notes one of you writes for the other to open at the right moment.

  • 🎯Our goals

    Daily, monthly and yearly goals you reach together.

  • 🎵Songs

    Your favourite songs in one place.

  • 🤫Secrets

    A space protected by a code on your phone.

Availability

Twin is in development and is currently in a closed test on Android devices, and we plan to launch it gradually on Google Play. The app is for people aged 18 or over.

Signing in

You sign in with an email address and password, or with your Google account. We never receive your password at Google. Sign-in with one of the providers may not work for every account at every time.

Your privacy

  • Your space is yours alone: your data is separated from every other couple's on the server.
  • Photos and recordings are kept in private, non-public storage and shown through temporary links.
  • Delayed messages and "Open When" notes are locked by the server itself, so your partner cannot read them before their time or before opening them.
  • No advertising and no analytics or crash-tracking tools in the app, and we do not sell your data.
  • Content is not end-to-end encrypted; it is stored on Twin's own project server.
  • You can delete your account or your shared space from inside the app.
