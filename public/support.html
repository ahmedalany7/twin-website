Frequently asked questions

Signing in and linking your partner

How do I sign in to Twin?

On the sign-in screen choose "Continue with Google" or "Continue with Facebook"; the browser opens, you pick your account, agree, and come back to Twin. You can also sign in with an email address and password if your account uses them. We never receive your password at Google or Facebook. If the button of one of the providers does not work for you, write to us.

How do I link my partner?

One of you creates the space and gets a time-limited invitation code; the other types it on the join screen. If both of you created an empty space, one of you enters the other's code to link them. A completed space cannot be joined by a third person.

I have an older email account. How do I add Google or Facebook to it?

Sign in with your older account, then: Settings → Account & data → "Ways to sign in" → "Link a Google account" or "Link a Facebook account". Linking adds a way to sign in to the same account; it does not change your space, your memories or your partner. There is no unlink today.

I signed in with Google or Facebook and found an empty account instead of my old one

The provider account's email differed from your older account's email, so a new account was created. Sign out and sign in with your older account by email, then link the provider from the settings. If you need help, write to us.

Deleting your account

How do I delete my account or our shared space?

In the app: Settings → Account & data. Deleting the account starts with a 7-day waiting period during which the account is locked and you can only cancel; after it ends the deletion starts and cannot be stopped or undone. Deleting the shared space needs both partners' agreement within 14 days. Full details: Delete your account and data.

I cannot get into my account and want to delete it

Write to us and say you want to delete your Twin account. We verify that you own the account in a secure way before any deletion, and we do not rely on simple non-secret questions alone. We do not delete an absent partner's account at the other person's request alone.

Is my Google or Facebook account deleted when I delete it in Twin?

No. Deleting your Twin account does not delete your account at the provider and does not withdraw Twin's permission there; you withdraw it from your account settings at the provider.

Technical problems

I cannot sign in

If you sign in by email, check the email and password. If you created your account with Google or Facebook, or linked it to one, use the same button and choose the same account (your Twin account has no password). If that does not work, write to us with the app version and your phone model.

How do I turn notifications off?

Settings → Notifications (the two reminder switches), and the "Thinking of you" notifications from your phone's settings.

Are our messages end-to-end encrypted?

No. Content is stored on Twin's own project server and is not end-to-end encrypted. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

Can I use Twin if I am under 18?

No. The app is for people aged 18 or over.
