2. What Twin is

A private app for exactly two people to keep their memories, messages, songs and games. A Twin space is shared by two people at most, and nobody can enter your space except by your invitation and acceptance of its code.

  • Delayed messages and "Open When" notes are hidden from the partner until their time or until the partner opens them.
