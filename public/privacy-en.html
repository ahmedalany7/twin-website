2. The idea of privacy in Twin

A Twin space belongs to two people only. There is no social network, no public profile and no sharing with strangers: what you add inside the space is visible to you and your partner only, and the database separates your data from every other couple's.

4. How we use your data

Only to run the app: sign you in, show your space, sync it between the two phones, send notifications, carry out deletion requests, and give support if you contact us. We do not sell your data and do not use it for advertising. We use content only to run Twin's services, including the AI processing, which works only with both partners' consent.

5. Providers we rely on

We use service providers to run the app, each with its own policy:

  • Supabase: database, authentication, storage, realtime connections and backend functions. All the data listed above is stored there, on servers in Ireland (region eu-west-1). We rely on the data processing agreement that Supabase publishes, which forms part of its terms of service, and on the safeguards it contains for transferring data.
  • Google (sign-in with a Google account): when you use it, the provider learns that you are signing in to Twin and processes your data on its own page under its own policy, not ours, then returns to Supabase what it shares: your name, email, profile picture link and account identifier. Deleting your Twin account does not delete your account at the provider and does not withdraw Twin's permission there; you withdraw it from your account settings at the provider.
  • Expo: the app-update service (the app contacts it to check for updates, sending the runtime version and platform) and the Expo Push Service.
  • Google (Firebase Cloud Messaging): delivers notifications to Android devices through Expo.
  • Google and Cloudflare (public STUN servers): when you use the direct transfer between your two devices in "Cinema", the STUN server (Google's or Cloudflare's) and your partner's device learn your device's public IP address.
  • Cloudflare (TURN relay): an optional feature that is not enabled in the current version of the app (the build settings do not switch it on); if it is enabled in the future, the encrypted connection data between the two devices passes through a relay, and we will update this page.
  • Open-Meteo (weather): only if you choose a weather city. The text you type to search for a city is sent to its geocoding service, and the approximate coordinates of your chosen city (rounded to two decimals) are sent to its weather service whenever your world shows the sky. As with any internet request, the service also sees your device's IP address. We send no account identifier, name or other content, and we do not store the answers. It works under its own terms and policy.
  • Groq: for the AI analysis (section 6).

We do not give your data to anyone else unless the law or a valid court order requires us to, and only to the extent required.

6. AI analysis of the "Daily question"

  • An optional feature that works only with both partners' consent; consent can be withdrawn at any time from the daily-question screen (this stops analyses that have not yet been sent). The feature is currently switched on at our server for all couples, but nothing is sent to Groq until both of you have agreed, and the server can switch it off at any time.
  • When it is on, the following is sent to Groq (model openai/gpt-oss-20b): the text of the daily question, the two partners' answers (up to 500 characters each) and the output language. We do not send your names, email, any identifier or any photo. What you write inside the answer itself is sent as written, so do not write anything in it that you do not want sent.
  • The model returns a short text and a compatibility score; they are saved in Supabase and shown to both of you. The secret key for Groq stays on our server and never reaches the app.
  • We do not log the text of your answers; we log only numbers and technical codes (request counts and usage).
  • The analysis is for fun only and is not psychological or relationship advice.
  • What Groq says about data on its side (according to its published documentation, the "Your Data" page): by default it does not retain customer data for inference requests; it may temporarily log inputs and outputs when troubleshooting errors that affect reliability or investigating suspected abuse, for up to 30 days unless the law requires longer; and it always collects usage metadata, which does not include inputs or outputs. We have switched on the Zero Data Retention option in our account with them, under which its documentation says it does not retain customer data for reliability and abuse monitoring. These are Groq's policies, not ours, and they may change, and we cannot guarantee that every trace of what was sent to it is deleted. Its documentation says that what it does retain is stored in the United States (see section 12).
  • What we do not know or control: the Groq documentation we have read does not state that your data is used to train models, and we do not control that. The no-retention setting belongs to our account at Groq and may change; we will update this page if it does. These requests may be processed in the United States (see the section on transfers). We do not guarantee the quality of the answer in Arabic or English, nor that the feature works when the service's quotas run out.

7. Security – what we do and what we do not

  • We connect to our servers over an encrypted connection (HTTPS).
  • Photos, recordings and video are kept in private, non-public storage and shown through temporary links (photos up to 7 days; recordings and video 1 hour).
  • Database isolation prevents any other couple from reaching your data.
  • Delayed messages and "Open When" notes are locked by the server so your partner cannot read them before their time or before opening them.
  • Your sign-in session is stored in your phone's secure store, and we never see your password.
  • Your private content is end-to-end encrypted once you both turn on "Encryption 🔒" on your phones (an optional trial feature): messages, memories, story events, "Secrets", songs and "Open When" notes, with their text, photos, recordings and videos. It is encrypted on your phone before it is sent, with a key that only your two phones hold, so neither we nor the server provider can read it. What was saved before you turned it on is encrypted with the "Encrypt the old content" button on the encryption screen, except "Open When" notes sent before you turned it on, which stay as they were sent. If one of you does not hold the key yet, the content is saved without this encryption so that it is never lost.
  • What is not end-to-end encrypted: games, questions, their answers and drawings, goals, the cinema, "Thinking of you" signals, your names and your start date, the dates, kinds and file names the app needs to order your content, and the daily-question analysis if you both agree to it. They are stored on Twin's own project at Supabase, and anyone with project administration access can technically reach them. We limit our access to what is technically or legally necessary and to the project's actual permissions, and we do not promise that they are never looked at.
  • If both your phones are lost and you did not create a recovery code, nobody can recover the encrypted messages, not even us.
  • The "Secrets" lock code protects the app screen on your phone only; it does not encrypt the content saved on the server.
  • No system is perfect: we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Device permissions

  • Photos, video and audio: to choose what you add to your memories, and to save a memory's photos or video into your phone's gallery when you press «Save to phone» (the files are written to your own device; if your gallery app backs them up to a cloud, that is under that app's policy, not ours); we read nothing else.
  • Microphone: to record voice messages inside the app only.
  • Notifications: for "Thinking of you" and gentle reminders.
  • Network: to reach the server.
  • Vibration and background operation: for app alerts. The app's manifest may declare extra permissions that the direct-connection library it relies on requires (such as the camera), and Twin does not use the camera.

You can withdraw any permission in your phone settings; only the related feature stops.

9. Retention and deletion

  • We keep your data as long as your account exists.
  • Deleting your account: in the app: Settings → Account & data → "Delete my account…". You re-enter your password (if your account is Google only you have no password: your last sign-in must be within the last 5 minutes, otherwise sign out and sign in again), then a 7-day waiting period starts during which the account is locked and you can only cancel. After it ends, the deletion starts to run and cannot be stopped or undone, and we do not set a time for it to finish.
    • Deleted: your sign-in account in Supabase Auth (email and password, if any), name, notification tokens, your presence, your answers in the games and daily question and analyses built on them, and unfinished "Open When" drafts. As for the records that link your account to Google at the provider itself, we do not control them, and we make no promise about what happens to the link records we hold or about whether you can create a new account with the same provider account after deletion.
    • Kept for your partner: what you uploaded or wrote in the shared space (memories, photos, video, messages, songs, "Open When" notes you sent), without your name. If you are the last member, the space is deleted with you.
  • Deleting the shared space: one of you requests it and the other agrees inside the app within 14 days, otherwise the request lapses and nothing is deleted. If no active partner remains, the remaining person can delete it alone. After agreement deletion starts immediately and removes all files and data.
  • Details and steps are on the deletion-request page.
  • If you cannot sign in to your account (you lost both your email and your password), contact support at mhdmoutazhammad@gmail.com; we verify that you own the account in a secure way before any deletion, and we do not rely on simple non-secret questions alone; we choose the method according to the case and set no fixed response time. When we schedule a deletion manually ourselves, we write to the email registered on the account. We do not delete an absent partner's account at the other person's request alone. In special cases (such as a deceased partner or one who lacks capacity) we review each case individually under the applicable law, and we promise no outcome.
  • Backups: we currently have no scheduled backups of the database (our current Supabase plan does not include them), so we do not restore deleted data from backups. The hosting provider's own infrastructure may keep internal copies for a period we do not control. We promise no specific period for this, and we will update this item if we move to a plan that includes backups.
  • We do not automatically delete inactive accounts at present. We may change this in the future by announcing it on this page.

10. Your choices and rights

  • See and correct your data: you can see your data in the app and change your name (and your password, if your account has one) under Settings → Account & data, and add Google to your account under "Ways to sign in" on the same screen (there is no unlink today).
  • Notifications: control reminders in Settings and system notifications on your phone.
  • Deletion: as in section 9.
  • A copy of your data, and access by others: there is no export feature in the app today, and we do not promise to add one. Requests for access or a copy (from the account owner or from anyone else) are reviewed under the law that applies to each case, and we reply within the time that law requires.
  • Your rights and the right to complain: you can ask for access to your data, or for it to be corrected or deleted, by writing to us, and we reply under the applicable law. You also have the right to complain to the competent authority in your country.

11. Children

Twin is not directed at anyone under 18 years old, and people under that age may not use it. We do not knowingly collect data from people under that age; if you know of such a case, contact us and we will delete it. The app itself does not check your age.

12. Transfers outside your country

Your data may be processed by our providers in countries other than yours (for example Ireland and the United States), and we rely on the safeguards each provider offers in its agreements.

13. Changes to this policy

If features or services change (for example switching on AI or a new service) we will update this page and its effective date.
